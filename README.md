# Hans ter Horst

I'm a Kubernetes engineer with 4 years of full-stack development experience. I hold the CKA, CKAD, KCSA, and KCNA, and I'm preparing for the CKS to become a Kubestronaut.

I started with Kubernetes in 2025 on a single K3s cluster. Since then, I've built a homelab cluster with three control-plane nodes and one worker node, and I've taken on real-world projects through KubeCraft's DevOps program, getting feedback from senior engineers. Most of my learning is hands-on: I run homelab clusters, break them, and rebuild them on purpose.

## What I work with

- **Kubernetes:** cluster setup, networking, security, and troubleshooting
- **GitOps:** ArgoCD and FluxCD, using Kustomize and Helm
- **Infrastructure as Code:** Terraform and Ansible
- **Cloud:** Azure AKS and DigitalOcean
- **CI/CD:** GitHub Actions, GitLab CI/CD, and Jenkins

## Projects

- [DevOps CI/CD Pipeline](https://github.com/hansth/k8s-gitops) (GitHub): Takes a Quarkus and React app from a devcontainer to Kubernetes, with an 80% coverage gate, Trivy image scans, and automated releases to GHCR.
- [Multi-Tenant AKS Platform](https://gitlab.com/HansTH/k8s-azure): Adding a customer to a Terraform list gives them their own n8n instance, database, secrets, and ingress, all deployed through FluxCD.
- [Rancher ArgoCD Pipeline](https://gitlab.com/HansTH/rancher-argocd): Multi-cluster management with Rancher and ArgoCD, with the servers provisioned by Terraform and configured by Ansible.
- [Kubernetes HomeLab](https://gitlab.com/homelab7894101/k8s-homelab): kubeadm HA cluster with Cilium (eBPF), Longhorn, etcd encrypted at rest, FluxCD, SOPS/age, Renovate, and Prometheus/Grafana.
